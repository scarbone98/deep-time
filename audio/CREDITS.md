# Audio credits

Both recordings are CC0 (public domain), from Freesound. Each was downmixed to mono and crossfaded into a seamless loop.

- `frogswamp.ogg`: "Froggy Swamp at Night" by flumpytripod. https://freesound.org/people/flumpytripod/sounds/828795/
- `darkrain.ogg`: "Dark Scape Forest rain" by szegvari. https://freesound.org/people/szegvari/sounds/580072/

Everything else is synthesized in `src/synth.gd`.

Checked by spectrogram: neither recording has birdsong. There were no birds in the Carboniferous.
